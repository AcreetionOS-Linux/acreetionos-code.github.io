Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 12, 2026

September 12, 2026

AcreetionOS CI now publishes ISOs automatically

The long‑standing geocode‑glib update conflict that broke installations on both acreetionos and acreetionos‑xlibre has finally been fixed. Community contributors merged the 'repair' branch and pushed a targeted update that restores the glib helper without breaking existing packages. Users on older systems can now upgrade cleanly and regain location services.

Development Natalie (@spivanatalie64) and Darren (@cobra3282000) have been polishing the CI pipeline, adding secret detection and SAST checks to .gitlab-ci.yml in natalie/my‑politecal‑stances. The recent commit in acreetionos‑code/acreetionos‑immutable passes -R to gh release create, which means every new build now ships an ISO straight to the release page without an extra checkout step. This automates publishing and reduces the window between code merge and a usable image, saving both maintainers and testers a few manual steps.

Community EuroPython 2026 videos are now live, featuring a talk from Natalie on building privacy‑first distributions; you can find them on the conference site. Meanwhile, Greg Kroah‑Hartman’s stable kernels 7.2.5 and 6.18.51 landed on Friday, and security updates from AlmaLinux, Debian, and Fedora addressed apr‑util, qt6‑qt5compat, libevent, ruby‑rack, bluez, corosync, curl and more. The community also posted a quick guide on applying the geocode‑glib fix on installed systems, so anyone stuck can get back online in minutes.

Tip of the day If you’re getting ready to try the latest build, grab the freshest ISO from our download page (https://acreetionos.org/flash.html) and follow the short install walk‑through in the wiki (https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html). For any questions or to follow the latest fixes, swing by the Discord and drop a line to Natalie or Darren—happy hacking!

Explore the new release details in our changelog (https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html) and see what’s happening in the dev logs on the homepage (https://acreetionos.org/).
