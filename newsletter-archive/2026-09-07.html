Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 07, 2026

September 07, 2026

Subject: Grml 2026.09 Released and AcreetionOS Knocks on Kernel 7.3-rc2's Door

Grml 2026.09 is out the door, and the AcreetionOS community is already celebrating one of the broader Linux ecosystem milestones of the week. Meanwhile, Darren and Natalie are keeping pace on the upstream kernel front, prepping 7.3-rc2 patches that will eventually land in our next stable build.

Development

Kernel prepatch 7.3-rc2 is the big ticket item this cycle. Both Natalie and Darren have been working to stay ahead of the mainline curve, which means when 7.3 stabilizes, AcreetionOS users should see it in our next ISO without the usual headache of chasing down patches ourselves. The Calamares config repo also picked up a couple of practical fixes from cobra3282000: Nvidia support got patched for older GPU families, and disk encryption was moved to an optional post-install step so new users aren't locked into it during their first boot if they are not ready. Small but user-friendly changes that keep the installer feeling smooth for everyone from newbies to veterans.

Community

The bash2bat conversion script saw its initial push this week, a handy utility for anyone bridging legacy shell scripts into Windows-friendly batch files. On the security front, the weekly patch review is tracking updates across Debian, Fedora, and the broader Arch tree, covering everything from Chromium and Firefox-ESR to kernel and Python-PIP fixes. Secret detection and SAST tooling were also added to the .gitlab-ci.yml pipelines in the community repos, which means we are getting a little more disciplined about catching leaked credentials before they hit the wild.

Tip of the Day

Want to grab the latest AcreetionOS ISO or see exactly what changed since the last release? Head over to https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html for the full rundown, or download a fresh build at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html. The wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html also has updated notes on the new Calamares encryption workflow.
